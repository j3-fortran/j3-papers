NUMBER: F23/022
TITLE: Are the procedures in ISO_FORTRAN_ENV simple?
KEYWORDS: ISO_FORTRAN_ENV, SIMPLE
DEFECT TYPE: Erratum
STATUS: J3 consideration in progress
REFERENCES: 24-007

QUESTION:

There appears to be no statement saying whether the procedures in the
standard intrinsic module ISO_FORTRAN_ENV are pure or simple.

The procedures are COMPILER_OPTIONS and COMPILER_VERSION.
These are not intrinsic procedures, and not inquiry functions.
However, they are permitted in constant expressions.

Are these procedures pure or simple?

ANSWER:

These procedures were intended to be simple (and thus also pure).
An edit is provided to correct this oversight.

EDIT to 24-007:

[458:7] 16.10.2.1 General
        (in 16.10.2 The ISO_FORTRAN_ENV intrinsic module),
        p2, after "not specific." append sentence
   "The procedures in the module are simple."
