1. Introduction
---------------

This paper contains edits to 17.9.220 THIS_IMAGE, paragraph 6, Examples Case
(ii).



The issues with this example are
  1. Performs a naive pull-broadcast through a coarray, instead of calling
     the CO_BROADCAST collective subroutine, which should be expected
     to provide more scalable performance at large image counts.
  2. P is never declared, and it would have to be a coarray for P[1] to work.
