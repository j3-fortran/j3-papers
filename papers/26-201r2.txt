1. Introduction

The statement saying that the procedures in the standard intrinsic module
ISO_FORTRAN_ENV are simple is misplaced and hard to find.

The procedures are COMPILER_OPTIONS and COMPILER_VERSION, plus four new
functions added in this revision. They should all say that they are simple
in their descriptions as well.
